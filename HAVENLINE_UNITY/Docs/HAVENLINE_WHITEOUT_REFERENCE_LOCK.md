## Locked visual/gameplay match points

- Close, readable orthographic/isometric camera.
- Strong portrait-first survivor readability at normal gameplay scale.
- Furnace/core heat as the visual and progression center of the camp.
- Clear warm-versus-cold lighting language.
- Automatic proximity interactions rather than excessive button prompts.
- Resources visibly carried by survivors.
- Deposits visibly transfer into storage/upgrades.
- Fast staged construction and visible world-state upgrades.
- Rescued survivors become active helpers.
- Compact minimal HUD that does not cover gameplay.
- Tight objective chains: gather -> carry -> deposit -> heat/build/comfort -> rescue/defend -> expand.
- Comfort/shelter upgrades are visible in the world.
- Threats, attacks and defense remain readable without turning the scene into a military shooter.
