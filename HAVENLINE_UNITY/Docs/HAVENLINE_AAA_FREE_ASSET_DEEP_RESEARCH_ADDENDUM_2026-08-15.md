Primary visual benchmark: approved Whiteout Survival gameplay segment.
Purpose: continue replacing the procedural/block-built prototype with commercially usable, genuinely higher-fidelity content.

## Promotion rule

A marketplace item is not promoted merely because its title says “AAA,” “realistic,” or “game ready.” It must have either an explicit current Free status under a usable marketplace license, CC0/public-domain status, or a clearly stated CC-BY license, and it must still pass the Havenline Unity shipping-camera audition.

For Fab acquisitions, retain entitlement evidence at download time. The Fab Standard License permits commercial/private use, modification, commercial distribution as part of a Project, and use in compatible tools beyond Unreal Engine; standalone redistribution is not allowed.

## Shipping-camera quality gate

Marketplace screenshots do not count as approval. Every promoted asset must be rendered inside Havenline at wide/close/phone/tablet/fold/night proof views. Reject assets that look excellent in an isolated viewer but read as noisy, tiny, plastic, mismatched, or still prototype-like in the actual Whiteout-style isometric camera.
